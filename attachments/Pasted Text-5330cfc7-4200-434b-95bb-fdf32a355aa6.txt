COMBAT / CAMERA / CHARACTERS / INVENTORY / LOOT FIX
Keep Diablo Immortal HUD layout and grim Sanctuary look. Lighting stay at the brighter readable grade already specified. Rebuild these systems.

════════════════════════════════════
1. CAMERA — ZOOM IN ON THE HERO
════════════════════════════════════
- Pull isometric camera IN. Hero should occupy \~18–24% of screen height (not a speck in a huge field).
- Frame: hero slightly below center. See full body + \~4–6m of ground around them. Packs read as individuals, not dots.
- Locked isometric. No free zoom. No RTS pull-back.
- Light follow-lag on dash/charge (120–180ms), then settle.
- Aiming a skill: optional 5–8% extra zoom-out for telegraph only, snap back on release.
- Keep HUD anchored to screen edges; do not scale HUD down just because the world zoomed in.

2. COMBAT FIX
════════════════════════════════════
Feel: weighty, fast, readable. Primary always available. Skills on cooldown only.

Targeting
- Primary hold = attack nearest enemy in a 90° facing cone, then nearest in 360° if cone empty.
- Stick/WASD steering turns the hero independently of auto-aim unless a skill is being aimed.
- Hold+drag skill shows a bright ground telegraph (circle / cone / line) and fires at release.
- Charge skills scale range/damage with hold time; full charge flashes the telegraph gold.
- Channel (Whirlwind): other skills lock with slash-overlay until channel ends.

Hit rules
- Melee needs a real volume, not a pixel. Cone / sphere checks.
- Packs stagger on heavy hits. Elites resist stun after 1 knockback / 3s.
- I-frames only on specified travel skills (leap midair, charge).
- Enemy telegraphs 0.7–1.1s, high-contrast red rim. Big hits never surprise from offscreen without a cue.

Juice
- 1–2 frame hitstop on slams / crits.
- Short camera punch on HoTA / leap land / elite death. No constant shake.
- Crit numbers larger + yellow. Normal white. Heals green.
- Potion: 2–3s heal-over-time, brief lockout, glow on hero. Cannot panic-chug every frame.

Enemy AI (minimum)
- Trash: approach, swing, light flinch.
- Ranged: stop at 8–10m, projectile with telegraph.
- Champion: 1 extra affix (fire nova / teleport / mortar) + nameplate icons.
- Boss: 3 attacks, 1 slam circle, 30% enrage.
- Leash: reset if hero runs \~25m from pack spawn.

Tuning
- Hero should clear a 8–12 trash pack in \~6–10s with primary + 2 skills.
- Getting hit matters: 4–6 trash hits or 2 champion hits should force a potion.
- No invincible standing-still DPS race. Movement always useful.

4. INVENTORY — DIABLO-STYLE EQUIP
════════════════════════════════════
Open from bag button. Full-screen dark stone + gold filigree. Large tap targets.

Layout
- LEFT: paperdoll. Same 3D hero, studio key+rim light, rotates \~15° on drag.
  Slot sockets around the body with metal frames:
  Head, Shoulders, Chest, Main Hand, Off Hand, Legs,
  Neck, Ring, Ring, Hands, Waist, Feet.
  Empty slot = engraved silhouette of that slot. Filled = item icon + rarity border.
- RIGHT: backpack grid, \~6×8 or 8×6 squares. Rarity-colored borders. Stack counts on gold/mats.
- TOP: gold / materials. Tabs: Equipment | Gems | Materials.
- BOTTOM or hover: tooltip.

Equip flow (must feel like Diablo)
- Tap/click item in grid → tooltip with Equip / Compare / Salvage.
- Double-tap or drop onto matching paperdoll slot to equip.
- Drag-and-drop onto slot or onto another item to swap.
- Equipping auto-unequips the old piece back to the first free grid cell.
- Invalid slot: snap back + error click. Do not eat the item.
- Compare: side-by-side vs equipped. Green ↑ / red ↓ on Damage, Life, Combat Rating, and 2–3 affixes.
- Locked items cannot salvage.
- Sort + filter by slot / rarity.

Tooltip (Diablo Immortal language)
- Item name in rarity color (white / magic blue / rare yellow / legendary orange).
- Slot + class.
- Big Damage or Life number.
- Affix list.
- Legendary power in gold paragraph if legendary.
- Required level / CR if any.

Paperdoll updates mesh/textures when gear changes (at least weapon + chest + helm visible).

5. LOOT DROPS — FIX
════════════════════════════════════
Spawn
- Every killed enemy can drop. Packs drop a mix. Elites guaranteed Magic+. Boss guaranteed Rare+ and a high chance of Legendary.
- Burst outward from corpse in a small ring, then settle. No stacking into one invisible pile.
- Gold is physical piles (small/med/large). Amount pops “+123 Gold”.
- Health globes separate from loot, walk-over pickup.

World representation
- Item = 3D mesh or high-res icon on a stand + rarity light shaft.
  White: faint. Blue: clear beam. Yellow: strong. Orange/gold: tall god-ray, sparks, sting sound.
- Nameplate above item: rarity-colored name + icon. Tap nameplate or walk over.
- Beams persist \~20s. Then despawn (or vacuum into bag if auto-loot includes that rarity).
- Never spawn loot inside walls, under the floor, or directly inside the corpse mesh. Offset to navmesh.

Pickup
- Settings: Auto-loot Off / White / Blue / Yellow. Legendaries default MANUAL.
- Manual: hand/interact button when standing on an item.
- Pickup range \~1.5m. Magnet only after explicit pickup or auto-loot rule.
- Inventory full: leave item on ground + “Inventory full” toast. Do not delete.

Feel
- Killing a pack should leave a readable scatter of gold + 2–6 items, not one shared sparkle.
- Legendary drop is an event: freeze-frame 4 frames, gold flash, unique audio, tall beam.
- Clicking loot never opens the wrong menu. Equip happens in inventory, not by eating the ground click.

GATE
1) Camera shows a detailed hero, not a minifig.
2) A pack fight has telegraphs, hitstop, and loot that lands on the floor as separate colored items.
3) Inventory can drag a yellow axe onto Main Hand and see it on the paperdoll + compare stats.
If any of those fail, fix that system before adding content.
